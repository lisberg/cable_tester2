| EXT | I(Rs) | P(Rs) | node voltage | share into IC ESD diodes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +24 V | 3.81 mA | 68 mW | 6.25 V | 3.67 mA |
| +30 V | 5.10 mA | 121 mW | 6.29 V | 4.77 mA |
| -24 V | -4.98 mA | 115 mW | -0.84 V | 4.67 mA |
| -30 V | -6.26 mA | 182 mW | -0.87 V | 5.64 mA |

Rs (1206, ≥ 0.25 W) covers the dissipation. A high‑Vf low‑leakage external clamp does **not** take the current away from the IC input diodes: most of it flows through the ICs. The bound is therefore Rs itself. Keep the per‑input injection below the IC absolute‑maximum clamp current (74HC: ±20 mA; TMUX1308: check datasheet), and make the VDD5 shunt clamp absorb the sum.
